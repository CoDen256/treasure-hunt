Welcome, treasure hunters.

Somewhere in this town, a trail is waiting — riddles, tasks, and puzzles scattered across streets you think you already know.

At each stop, solve what's in front of you. Work together! But some tasks were built for one set of eyes only.

Solve it, verify it, snap a photo, and let the clues point you onward.

Everyone solves. But choose three among you to also carry a role: the Lead, who presents each tasks to everyone and verifies the answers; the Navigator, who charts the way there; and the Keeper of Moments, who captures each stop before the group moves on.

Piece by piece, stop by stop, a final treasure awaits at the end of it all.

Good luck. Stay curious.

Clue #1  ·  Riddle
The Woman Without a Crown
This riddle names a real place in this city. Solve it — then go there.
Seek out the woman who wears no crown,
Yet rules a palace here in town.
Not a castle of stone and moat,
But one where flickering stories float.
Her name means "queen" in a language old,
Her halls are dark, her tales are bold.
Where we sit, we watch, we later debate —
One studio brings us stories of fate,
Its number is twenty, and four the rest,
The strangest, boldest, and often the best.
