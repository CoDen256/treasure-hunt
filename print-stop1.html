The First Envelope

Station I of IX
All seven together
Postmark — A letter to all of you

Welcome, treasure hunters.

Tonight isn't an ordinary evening. Somewhere in this town, hidden in places that mean something — some to all of you, some to only one of you — lies a trail. Follow it well, and it will lead you somewhere unexpected. Follow it together, and it will lead you somewhere true.

This is not a race. There are no winners, no losers — only the seven of you, a handful of clues, and a town that's about to feel a little more like a storybook than you're used to.

At each stop, you'll find tasks waiting for you — some for the whole group to puzzle over together, and one meant only for a certain someone, who may know things the rest of you don't. Solve them, and they'll give you fragments: a word, a number, a symbol, a feeling.

Piece those fragments together, and they'll form a clue — sometimes a riddle, sometimes something stranger. Solve that, and you'll know where to go next.

When you think you've found the answer, check it at the link at the bottom of this page — it will confirm if you're right, or nudge you gently if you're not.

Then walk. Then look. Then arrive — and see what's waiting.

There is one final stop tonight. What's there, none of you know yet. But she might, if she pays close enough attention.

Good luck. Stay curious. And enjoy the walk.

The Notes — Your tasks for this stop
Note 01  ·  Group
The Palace Without a Crown
Seek out the woman who wears no crown,
Yet rules a palace here in town.
Not a castle of stone and moat,
But one where flickering stories float.
Her name means "queen" in a language old,
Her halls are dark, her tales are bold.
Note 02  ·  Group
The Studio of Strange Tales
Where we sit, we watch, we later debate —
One studio brings us stories of fate,
Its number is twenty, and four the rest,
The strangest, boldest, and often the best.
The name of this studio will be needed when you arrive at the palace the first riddle named.
Note 03  ·  Group
The Scattered Word
A word has been scattered
like ash in the wind:
YESYODS
Unscramble it.
Piece it back together, and you'll hold the second key.

The word you find is not the answer — it is the key. Return to the palace the first riddle named. Carry your unscrambled word with you — it will guide your eyes once you arrive.
