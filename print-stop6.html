The Method — Trilateration

Each riddle below points to a real place in Leipzig. Solve all three — then, on the map overleaf, find each location and draw a circle around it using the given radius and the scale bar. The place where all three circles meet is your next destination.

Circle 1

Where settlers claim their roads and sheep,
And strategy is ours to keep,
Not a toy shop, but something more —
A kingdom's worth behind that door.

Roll the dice, and draw a card,
This little shop's not hard to spot —
Where board and box and rulebook meet,
Just steps away, along the street.

While pictures formed of bone too soon.
Name the place where they could see —
The hidden truth inside her knee.
